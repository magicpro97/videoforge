✨ Features

🎬 Text to Video

Generate videos from text prompts across 5 AI providers with style presets.

📷 Image to Video

Animate still images into video clips with motion prompts and camera control.

🎨 15 Style Presets

cinematic, animation, vfx, anime, pixel-art, glitch, 3d-render, and more.

📐 11 Resolutions

480p to 4K, plus phone, tiktok, youtube, story, and square presets.

📦 Batch Processing

Generate multiple videos from YAML configs in one command.

📊 Cost Tracking

Track spending per provider with detailed summaries and pricing info.
